# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address or email, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
channel — and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at
[contact@stringutilitybelt.com](mailto:contact@stringutilitybelt.com). Every report will be reviewed
promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct, and may warn, temporarily ban or
permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge inappropriate, following the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
